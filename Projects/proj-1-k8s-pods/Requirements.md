## Project 01 — **Create Your First Pod**

### What you will learn

- Pods
- YAML manifests
- kubectl
- Pod lifecycle

### Tasks

Create a Pod called: `web-po` using: `nginx:alpine`

### Requirements

1. Create the Pod using YAML.
2. Apply the manifest.
3. Verify that the Pod is running.
4. Inspect the Pod.
5. View its logs.
6. Delete the Pod.
7. Verify its removal.
